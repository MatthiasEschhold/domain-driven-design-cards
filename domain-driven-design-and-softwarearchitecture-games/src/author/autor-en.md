# Author

I’m Matthias Eschhold – Software Architect, Team Lead, and dedicated Domain-Driven Design practitioner.
For more than a decade, I’ve been driven by a question that has become central to what I consider good software development:
How can we design software architecture in a way that not only supports a company’s domain, but truly reflects it?

This question led me to Domain-Driven Design. DDD fascinates me because it goes far beyond architecture.
To me, it is a mindset that connects technical, architectural, and social dimensions:

On a social level, it creates a shared language, strengthens collaboration, and helps teams think together more effectively.

On an architectural level, it enables loose coupling, clear responsibilities, and evolutionary system development.

On a technical level, it provides patterns that lead to sustainable, domain-oriented codebases.

Throughout my career, I’ve designed and evolved architectures for complex business systems, 
built and led cross-functional teams, helped shape IT and organizational structures, and guided modernization initiatives 
for large-scale IT landscapes.

Today, I work as a Lead Architect and Team Lead at EnBW AG. My focus is on fostering an architecture culture that 
relies less on rules and more on shared principles. I believe teams need clear guardrails—but also enough autonomy 
to make their own decisions and take ownership. For me, an architecture is successful when it provides direction 
without limiting creativity or responsibility.

In addition to my primary role, I am an iSAQB-certified trainer for Software Architecture and Domain-Driven Design.
I also created the training “Evolutionary Architecture Patterns with Clean and Hexagonal Architecture,” 
where I teach how modern architectures emerge, grow, and remain sustainable over time.
