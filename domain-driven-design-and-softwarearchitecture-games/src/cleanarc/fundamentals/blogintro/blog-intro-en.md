# Clean Architecture, Domain-centric Architecture Patterns & Domain-Driven Design

In this blog about Clean Architecture, domain-centered architectural patterns, and Domain-Driven Design,
I share the knowledge and experience I've gained over many years as an architect and trainer —
continuously refined, expanded, and improved.

I will cover both fundamental principles and advanced concepts, enriched with real-world stories
to provide a solid and practical understanding of Clean Architecture.

The foundation of this blog is the introductory section.
Building on that, I will iteratively explore deeper topics, advanced techniques,
and concrete examples from real-world projects.

Stay tuned for the insights, inspirations, and proven practices that will be collected here over time!
